About my work

Statistical methods. Health questions.

I hold a Ph.D. in Mathematical Statistics and am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Tampere University, Finland. Previously, I worked at Zhejiang University and Zhengzhou University, China, contributing to research in health data science, infectious disease epidemiology, and predictive modeling using UK Biobank and clinical data.
